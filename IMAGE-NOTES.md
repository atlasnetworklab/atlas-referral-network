> Scene/backdrop: A charcoal slate production display surface with a calm slate studio background.

> Materials/textures: Matte and subtly textured paperboard, soft pouch film, brushed aluminum, opaque pump-bottle plastic, smooth tube material, visible edges and grain of paper packaging swatches.
> Constraints: Original generic unbranded packaging shapes. No text, lettering, numbers, labels, logos, trademarks, watermarks, or alcohol bottles. No people. Do not copy recognizable branded products. Produce exactly one image.
